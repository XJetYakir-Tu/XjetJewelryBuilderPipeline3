# JewelryB2C3 — product, UX and technical scan with ideas (3 October 2026)

**Scope.** A fresh pass over the live site and Admin on proto (commit `102ee62`), the code and the server, after the team review of 2 October and the work done since (typography, phone Design screen, Journey images, metal colours). This note adds ideas rather than repeating fixed items; open items from the earlier review that still matter are carried into the final list.

**Fresh facts used below**

- Media on proto: 20 movies, 4.9 MB each on average (768p, 6 s), served without cache headers and played with `autoplay loop` and no poster; 96 candidate images, 713 KB each on average, full size everywhere. PIL on the server can write WebP; ffmpeg is not installed on the server (it is on the dev machine).
- Delivery: HTTP only, no HTTP/2; the page's HEAD returns 405; no `robots.txt` or sitemap; three third-party hosts on every page load (Google Fonts, unpkg for Alpine, the Tailwind CDN).
- Admin: one shared admin key, so every action is signed "developer-key".

**Reference patterns** (what the strong sites do, from experience rather than a live audit): James Allen's 360° HD product video as *the* product image; Holden's live wedding-band configurator with instant preview and price; Taylor & Hart's bespoke flow that shows the sketch → render → finished piece story; Mejuri's editorial, airy product pages with price and delivery always visible; Tiffany and Cartier's full-bleed, slow, confident hero media with one line of copy; Tesla's and Apple's configurators with a sticky summary and one primary action per step; Midjourney, Runway and Krea landing pages that *show the product working* in a fast muted loop within the first second.

## 1. The hero showcase

**Recommendation: build the hero "video" as a live, scripted sequence in the page, not as a video file first.** It uses the real renders and 360° movies the site already has (zero AI cost), stays razor-sharp at every screen size, respects reduced-motion, can switch metals with the real colour filters, and can be exported to an MP4 later for social media. A file-based video would need to be re-cut every time the gallery or the brand copy changes.

Length 9–10 seconds, muted, loops, pauses on hover, replaced by the current rotating ring for people who prefer reduced motion. Six beats, fast cuts, no narration, one short caption per beat in the eyebrow style:

| # | Beat | What is on screen | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Describe it* | The composer with a prompt typing itself: "a slim band wrapped by a serpent" | 1.4 s |
| 2 | *Four designs in a minute* | Four real gallery renders pop in, one after another | 1.6 s |
| 3 | *Refine* | One render crossfades into its refinement (a real Original → Variation pair) | 1.2 s |
| 4 | *See it turn* | A real 360° movie plays, large | 2.4 s |
| 5 | *Choose your metal* | The same movie switches silver → rose gold → 18K gold with the live filters | 1.8 s |
| 6 | *Made in real metal by XJet* | A photo of a printed ring / the NanoParticle Jetting part, then the CTA | 1.4 s |

Beat 6 needs one real photograph of a printed ring (or a short clip from the XJet factory). Everything else can be built now. On phones the sequence replaces the missing hero visual in the first screen. A one-click "Watch again" and the CTA sit under it.

## 2. Ideas by area

### Customer UX
- **Design first, account later.** Let a visitor type the prompt or paste a photo before signing in; ask for the email when the four images are about to be generated and keep the prompt. One sign-in flow (a code typed on the same screen), not two.
- **Lower the blank-page barrier.** Prompt chips under the composer ("Minimal twisted band", "Signet with a hexagon face", "Snake wrap ring", "Something like this photo…"), and a two-line "what makes a good brief" hint.
- **"Start from a photo" as a first-class entry** on the homepage and in the hero, since pasting and uploading already work.
- **Sizing help where the decision is made:** a printable ring sizer PDF, EU/UK/JP conversions next to US sizes, and the line "We confirm the size with you before production" (the reservation model already allows it).
- **Favourites and history:** a heart on options, and a "My Designs" entry that shows the last four options at a glance.

### Premium presentation
- The hero sequence above; one line of copy, large media, calm pacing.
- A **"The craft" page** with real production imagery and a 20-second factory clip (content from XJet), replacing the thin Technology page.
- **Material naming with dignity:** "Sterling & Steel" and "Gold — made to quote" instead of "Fashion Jewelry / Luxury".
- Consistent product imagery: every render on white, same framing; the thumbnails pipeline below makes them crisp at every size.

### Conversion
- **Payment.** Stripe Checkout behind the existing seam, with Apple Pay and Google Pay; an **express "Buy now"** from Customize for a single ring; the order stays "new" until the webhook confirms, exactly today's model.
- **Price and delivery visible early:** "From $90 in steel" on gallery tiles, "Ships in 7–10 business days · 2-year warranty" under the price in Customize and in the bag.
- **Recovery loops:** three automatic emails (a design left unfinished, a bag with a ring, a payment pending) with a one-click return link.
- **Gift path:** gift note, a sizing guide for gifts, and later a gift card.

### Mobile
- The phone Design screen is now a swipeable row with a sticky composer; next: a **bottom-sheet metal picker** in Customize, and the hero sequence in the first screen.
- Every image and movie at mobile sizes (see media pipeline); the first screen under 300 KB.

### Gallery
- **Tags and filters** once the gallery passes ~12 designs (band, signet, statement, stackable; metal; price), a "New" badge, and a one-line story per design (the prompt excerpt, with the customer's consent).
- **Shareable rings:** a Share button in the lightbox, a stable `?ring=R-1013` link, Open Graph image and title for previews on WhatsApp and Instagram; the Admin's "Copy link" hands out the same link.
- Show the four options behind a gallery design *before* sign-in as a teaser.

### Design / Customize
- **Finish option** (polished / brushed / matte) and **engraving** (inner band text) are the two most requested custom-ring features; both need a manufacturing decision on what NanoParticle Jetting supports and at what cost.
- A customer-facing **3D turntable** once a model exists (the Admin already has a light preview) — a paid step, so a business decision on who pays for a model before an order.
- Keep the 360° movie as the pre-order preview; make its first frame instant (poster) and its file small (below).

### Checkout
- Payment and express checkout (above); **address autocomplete and validation** behind the existing seam; a **tax and duties estimate** per country before marketing abroad; an **order status page** with emails on Payment confirmed, In production and Shipped (carrier + tracking).

### Trust and social proof
- Real photos of printed rings and the factory; a "made by XJet" line at every decision point; warranty and returns summarised near the price; pilot-customer reviews once there are any; an "as featured in" row when there is press. All content-dependent.
- A **support channel that fits the market:** a WhatsApp "Ask a designer" button with a stated response time.

### Technical performance, loading and media
- **Image pipeline:** write 320 px and 800 px WebP thumbnails when a candidate becomes ready (and once for the 96 existing images); `srcset` everywhere; `Cache-Control: immutable` for `/assets`; `gzip_types` for JS and JSON. The homepage drops from ~4 MB to well under 1 MB.
- **Movie pipeline:** a poster JPEG per movie for an instant first frame, a 480p variant for phones, `preload="metadata"` until the Movie tab is chosen, and cache headers. Needs ffmpeg on the server (apt) or the `imageio-ffmpeg` wheel.
- **Delivery:** HTTPS and HTTP/2, security headers, HEAD support on the page, `robots.txt` and a sitemap, a server-rendered landing page for search engines (the SPA is invisible to them today).
- **Self-host the three third-party dependencies** (fonts, Alpine, a built Tailwind): privacy (no Google Fonts call from the EU), reliability, and a faster first paint.
- **Backups and monitoring** remain the top infrastructure gap: nightly off-box copies of the database and assets, uptime and disk alerts.

### Admin / operations
- **Staff accounts** (name + key or code) so the audit trail says who did what, with a simple role split (operations vs. settings).
- **Production paperwork:** a printable production sheet per order (ring, option, material, size, STL name, QR to the order), a "to print this week" list, CSV export.
- **Margin per order:** AI cost of the session plus the 3D production cost estimate against the price, on the order page and the dashboard.
- **Customer messaging from the order page** (templates: payment instructions, size confirmation, shipped) through the existing mail transport.
- **Retention policy** for images, movies and meshes of designs that were never ordered; the system page already shows the numbers.
- **Alerts:** failed emails, provider errors, disk, review backlog older than N days.

### Still missing
- **Analytics** beyond the internal funnel (a privacy-friendly, self-hosted tool) and a light A/B mechanism for the hero and the sign-in moment.
- **Manufacturing correctness:** sintering shrinkage compensation per material and minimum wall/feature checks before the first paid print.
- **Markets:** landed prices (VAT/sales tax), currencies and Hebrew/RTL when the market list is decided.
- **Legal hygiene:** a short accessibility statement; cookie notice is not needed while there are no third-party trackers, and self-hosting fonts keeps it that way.
