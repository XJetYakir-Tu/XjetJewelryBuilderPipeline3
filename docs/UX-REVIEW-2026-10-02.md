# JewelryB2C3 — UI / UX review (2 October 2026)

**Scope:** the customer site `http://proto/JewelryB2C3/` and the Admin `http://proto/JewelryB2C3/admin/`, as deployed (`cda5f11`). Reviewed live at 1366 px, 768 px and 375 px; the Design → Customize → Bag screens on the local mock server (same code), because proto runs in paid mode. Facts below come from the pages themselves and the source (`web/index.html`, `web/admin.html`, `web/app.js`, `web/admin.js`).

This is a design review, not a feature list: what a first-time customer or a daily admin experiences, and what to change first.

---

## 1. Summary

**Customer site.** The brand layer is strong — serif headlines, restrained gold, generous whitespace, a calm four-step story. The design flow itself is clear and the new gallery gives the page a real product to show. The weak points are below the surface of the hero: the site does not show a ring above the fold, phones have no way to reach five of the six pages, secondary text is set in 9–11 px light grey (99 occurrences of 10–11 px type), the primary action on Customize sits below the fold, and the vocabulary leaks internal terms ("generations", "token", "Exit"). Several pages are a paragraph long.

**Admin.** Functionally rich and honest about data, with good patterns already in place (colour-coded badges, relative + absolute times, real progress states, confirmations before paid actions). It reads as a developer tool: 11 px uppercase labels everywhere (72 occurrences of 11 px, 14 of 10 px, 7 of 9 px), 12-column tables without sticky headers, a session page that scrolls through nine sections with the main task near the bottom, 17 native `alert()`/`confirm()`/`prompt()` dialogs for feedback, and no keyboard focus styles. Nothing is broken; it is simply harder and slower to use than it needs to be, and a non-technical colleague would struggle.

The highest-value changes are small: a mobile menu, a ring in the hero, larger secondary type, the Customize CTA above the fold, a sticky summary bar on the session page, and replacing native dialogs with inline confirmations and toasts.

## 2. Customer site

### 2.1 First impression (landing)

| # | Finding | Why it matters | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **The hero shows a sculpture, not a ring.** The angel figurine is beautiful but the product is rings; the first ring appears only after two screens of scrolling. | A visitor decides in seconds whether this is for them; the hero promises jewellery design and shows something else. | Put a real gallery ring (or a short loop of the 360° movie) in the hero, with the angel moved to the Technology section as proof of what the printers can do. |
| C2 | **"Currently supported: Rings only" appears twice** on the landing page, plus a "coming soon" note for pendants in the gallery intro. | Three apologies for what the product is *not* dilute what it *is*. | Say it once, positively: "Rings — the first XJet Atelier collection". |
| C3 | **Secondary text is light grey (#8F8F8F / #6F6F6F) at 10–12 px** — the process cards, the gallery captions (10 px uppercase), hints under the form. | Contrast of #8F8F8F on white is ~3.5:1 — below the 4.5:1 needed for small text; on phones it is hard to read outdoors. | Body hints ≥ 13 px, captions ≥ 11 px, grey no lighter than #6B6B6B for text that carries meaning. Keep the light grey for decoration only. |
| C4 | The **production / delivery claims** block (same day, 7–10 days, 5 days express, 2-year warranty) reads as a commitment next to a "preview" disclaimer. | Mixed signals: either it is a shop or a preview. | Keep the block, but add "typical" and move it closer to the Bag, where it is relevant. |

### 2.2 Navigation

| # | Finding | Why it matters | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| C5 | **On phones there is no menu.** The six page links (How it works, Inspiration, Materials, Technology, FAQ, About) are hidden below 768 px with no hamburger; the footer offers only Shipping, Terms, Privacy, Contact and Developer. | Five pages are unreachable for roughly half the visitors. | A hamburger menu, or a compact footer navigation with all pages. |
| C6 | **"Developer" is in the customer footer** and opens an admin-key prompt. | Customers see internal controls (also flagged in the product review). | Move the developer entry to the Admin; remove the link. |
| C7 | The top bar in the studio shows "DESIGN — CUSTOMIZE — BAG" as a step indicator, but the current step is only marked by an underline, and the words are also the only way back. | Users miss the underline; the hierarchy between "where am I" and "where can I go" is flat. | Use a proper stepper: numbered steps, the completed ones ticked, the current one filled; keep them clickable. |
| C8 | **"EXIT"** in the top right signs the customer out. | "Exit" suggests leaving the studio, not logging out; some users will lose their session by accident. | Label it "Sign out", in the account menu, with a confirmation only if a design is mid-generation. |

### 2.3 Sign-in and vocabulary

| # | Finding | Why it matters | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| C9 | The dialog title is "Welcome / XJet Atelier Access"; the alternative is "Already have a token? ### 2.4 Design screen (studio)

What works: four large options, the waiting movie, the "Selected" tag, My Designs with thumbnails and the Gallery tag, the Inspiration Gallery button, the "Started from the Inspiration Gallery" label.

| # | Finding | Why it matters | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| C12 | The small black **"ORIGINAL"** pill top-left is the batch switch (Original / Refinement 1…), but it looks like a status label. | The one control that switches between batches is not recognisable as a control. | Render batches as tabs ("Original · Refinement 1 · Refinement 2") under the headline. |
| C13 | The composer at the bottom changes meaning (new design vs refine the selected one) with only a placeholder text difference. | Users refine when they meant to start fresh, or vice versa. | A visible mode toggle above the box: "Refine the selected design" / "Start a new design", and refinement chips ("thinner band", "add a stone", "matte finish"). |
| C14 | The prompt bubble repeats the full prompt (on gallery designs, XJet's internal prompt). | Noise; the long prompt pushes the images down on phones. | Truncate to two lines with "more"; for gallery designs show only the design name. |
| C15 | Hover zoom on option cards exists, but there is no "compare" view. | Choosing between four similar rings is the core decision. | A simple side-by-side zoom (two options) or a larger single-option view with arrows. |

### 2.5 Customize

What works: Image/Movie toggle, the metal colour preview, the size grid, a clear summary.

| # | Finding | Why it matters | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| C16 | **"Add to Bag" is below the fold** at 1366×850 and on every phone: Metal type → Size → Summary → Price → Quantity → CTA. | The primary action is not visible when the page opens. | A sticky summary bar (price · size · material · Add to Bag) at the bottom of the right column / bottom of the phone screen. |
| C17 | The price area still says **"Based on an assumed volume of 1 cm³ · est. 10.36 g of metal (assumed, not measured)"**; the Bag says "per piece at an assumed volume of 1 cm³". | Leftover engineering copy now that prices are a fixed table; it makes the price look uncertain. | "Fixed price for Silver. Final weight is measured at production." |
| C18 | Luxury gold is "PREVIEW ONLY" with "available to order soon" and no path forward. | The most valuable customer segment hits a dead end. | "Request a quote" button on luxury materials (email to sales with the ring ID). |
| C19 | The size grid has 17 equal buttons and a "Size guide" link; no hint of the customer's usual size. | Picking a US size without help is the most error-prone step. | Keep the grid, add the mm equivalent on the selected size and make the size guide an inline sheet. |
| C20 | After "Add to Bag" the page stays put; only the bag counter changes. | No confirmation of success. | A toast "Added to your bag · View bag", or slide the bag in. |

### 2.6 Bag

| # | Finding | Why it matters | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| C21 | "CHECKOUT UNAVAILABLE" is the only primary-looking action; the explanatory paragraph is grey and small. | A dead end at the point of highest intent (8 of 10 sessions reach the bag). | "Request this ring" (order request by email with ring ID, size, material, price), and make the explanation the lead text, not a footnote. |
| C22 | The bag line shows a tiny thumbnail and the design name; the ring ID and the chosen option are not shown. | Customers cannot refer to the ring when they write to you. | Show the ring ID (R-1013-A) on the line and in the request email. |

### 2.7 Content pages

Materials (164 characters of text), Technology (250), Inspiration intro (219) and Contact (331) are a paragraph each; FAQ has 10 good items; About XJet (1,754) and Shipping & Returns (963) are reasonable; Terms and Privacy are marked "Draft pending legal review".

- **Materials** should be the page that sells the metals: a swatch, density, finish, care, price band per material (the data exists in the Admin table).
- **Technology** should carry the sculpture and printer imagery, not the hero.
- **Inspiration** page now duplicates the home gallery — fine, but give it filters (metal, style) once there are 20+ designs.

### 2.8 Phone

No horizontal overflow at 375 or 360 px, tap targets are adequate, the hero stacks well, the gallery grid is two columns. Besides the missing menu (C5): the step bar wraps awkwardly at 360 px, and the gallery lightbox's "Back to gallery" is below the fold on small phones — put the two buttons side by side.

### 2.9 Accessibility and feedback

- Two hero images have no `alt`; focus styles exist (14 `focus:` rules) but the gold-on-white focus ring is faint.
- One native `confirm()` (remove from My Designs) — acceptable; prefer an inline "Remove? Yes / No".
- Error text is red at 14 px (fine); loading states are text only — a skeleton for the four option cards would read as faster.

## 3. Admin

### 3.1 Information architecture and navigation

| # | Finding | Why it matters | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **The session page is one long scroll of nine sections** (summary, artifacts, customers on this design, pipeline, 3D production, last choice, designs, journey). The daily task — check a session, run or recalculate 3D, download the STL — sits in section five. | Every visit costs two or three screens of scrolling; the eye has no anchor. | A sticky header with the customer, ring ID, stage and three actions (Generate / Recalculate 3D · Download STL · Show in gallery), plus a right-hand section index (anchors). Collapse "Designs" and "Journey" by default like "What was done". |
| A2 | Breadcrumb "Sessions / The Orion Ring" exists, but going back loses the list's filters and scroll position. | Triage of 20 sessions becomes 20 round trips. | Keep filters in the URL hash and restore scroll on back; add "← Previous / Next session" on the detail page. |
| A3 | The admin key is asked for **per browser tab** (sessionStorage) and again for Developer tools. | Friction several times a day; teaches people to paste the key everywhere. | Remember for the browser (localStorage with a 12-hour expiry), one sign-in for Admin and developer tools. |
| A4 | Tab labels: "AI Prompts & Params" is a developer phrase; the material pricing table lives under it. | Non-technical colleagues will not look for prices under "prompts". | "Settings" with sub-tabs "Pricing", "AI models", "AI prices"; or a separate "Pricing" tab. |

### 3.2 Typography, density, tables

| # | Finding | Why it matters | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A5 | **72 uses of 11 px, 14 of 10 px, 7 of 9 px**, most of them uppercase with wide tracking (`lbl`). Table cells are 12 px. | Uppercase micro-type is slow to read and tiring across a full day; it also makes the pages look "unfinished". | Labels 12 px sentence case (uppercase only for section titles), table body 13 px, numbers tabular (already). |
| A6 | The Sessions table has **12 columns** with no sticky header; at 1280 px it needs horizontal scrolling, at 768 px cells wrap into three lines. | Heavy scanning; the key fields (customer, stage, 3D, price) compete with secondary ones (Gen/Ref, Checkout clicked). | A two-line row: line 1 customer · ring ID · stage path · price; line 2 chips (Image, Movie, 3D, Bag, Mock, Gallery). Secondary columns behind a "Columns" menu. Sticky header; column sort. |
| A7 | 15 tables, none with sticky headers or zebra rows; action links ("Copy link", "↑ ↓", "Remove") are 11 px text. | Easy to click the wrong row; tiny targets. | Sticky headers, subtle zebra rows, 32 px icon buttons with tooltips for actions. |

### 3.3 Dashboard

What works: the five KPI cards, the funnel bars, the by-origin split, the storage card, the 3D tables with the fixed-price basis row, the activity log.

| # | Finding | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| A8 | No time range: every number is "all time" since 1 Oct. | A range picker (7 / 30 / 90 days / all) at the top, applied to all cards. |
| A9 | The page is long and all sections have equal visual weight. | Order by decision value: KPIs → funnel → gallery top-3 → 3D accuracy → storage → activity; make the geometry table collapsible. |
| A10 | "Fixed vs 3D price −25.6%" is a single number with no direction or target. | Show it as "3D price is 25.6% below the fixed price" with the per-material split, since the table already has it. |

### 3.4 Sessions list and detail

| # | Finding | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| A11 | Filters are good (stage, bag, 3D, mock, search) but there is no "needs attention" view (3D needs review, generation failed, checkout clicked). | A default saved filter "Needs attention" and a count badge on the Sessions tab. |
| A12 | The 3D production form repeats three labels ("Customer selected size", "3D production size", "Material") above three fields with small helper lines, then the amber existing-model panel, then the results. | Group as one card: "Model: R-1013-A (ready) · Size [US 9] · Material [Silver] · [Recalculate geometry]" with the safeguard link in the corner; results below as a compact table (one row per size/material) with Download on each row. |
| A13 | The live stage line ("Ready — Total 05:05 · Waiting for Hi3D 00:09 · …") is excellent; the result card's eight metrics are set in 11 px labels. | Promote the four numbers that matter (inner diameter, weight, production cost, 3D price) to 16 px; keep the rest as details. |
| A14 | The journey is a clear timeline; relative times are shown in a dim column. | Good as is; add a filter "customer actions only / include admin". |

### 3.5 Gallery, Users, Pricing

| # | Finding | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|
| A15 | Gallery tab: a strong statistics table; the three "Most …" cards show only the winner. | Make each card a mini top-3 and link to the drill-down. Add the tile image to the cards. |
| A16 | Users: the full access code is shown in the list (by design); "Edit / Deactivate / Remove / Restore" are text links. | Keep the code behind a "Reveal" click; use icon buttons with a confirm for Remove. |
| A17 | Pricing · materials: clear table; "Edit" turns every cell into an input at once. | Fine for 9 rows. Add "Last changed by … on …" per row and a "preview impact" line (how many bag lines would change). |

### 3.6 Feedback, dialogs, accessibility

| # | Finding | Why it matters | Suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A19 | **7 `confirm()`, 9 `alert()`, 1 `prompt()`** — native browser dialogs for confirmations, errors and the share link. | They look foreign, cannot be styled, block the page, and show up as "localhost says". | One inline confirmation pattern (the row turns into "Remove this design? Yes / Cancel") and a toast component for success/error. The typed-confirmation popup for a new Hi3D model is the right pattern — reuse it for the other destructive actions. |
| A20 | No `aria-label` and no `focus:` styles anywhere in the Admin (0 and 0); tables are unlabeled. | Keyboard users cannot see where they are; screen readers get bare icons. | Visible focus ring, labels on icon buttons, `<caption>`s on tables. |
| A21 | Timestamps are shown in the viewer's local time without saying so; the server works in UTC. | Two admins in different zones read different times. | State the timezone once in the header ("Times shown in your local time, UTC+3"). |

2. A ring in the hero (C1); say "Rings" once (C2).

## 5. What is already good — keep it

- The brand system (Cinzel + Inter, gold accents, whitespace) is consistent across both sites.
- Honest states everywhere: "Price unavailable", "Needs review", real progress with timers, "mock" clearly marked.
- The waiting movie, the four-option grid, the metal colour preview and the 360° toggle make the studio feel premium.
- Admin badges are colour-coded consistently (stage, status, origin), times are absolute + relative, destructive and paid actions ask first, and the new typed confirmation for a second Hi3D model is exactly the right level of friction.
- The gallery, with real rings and "Make it yours", finally shows the product on the landing page.
